Didit is a habit tracker built for your home screen. It isn't about streaks or pass/fail goals. It shows what you actually did, so a glance at your launcher tells you how the last two weeks went.

Four ways to see a habit:

• One of: where did you eat today? Home, takeout or out. Each pick is a coloured dot, stacked by day.
• Amount: pages read, minutes run. A bar for each day, scaled to your usual.
• Count: cups of coffee, glasses of water. A tally for each day.
• Done: walked, meditated, stretched. A filled dot, or an empty ring.

The widget:

• Every habit as a row over the last 14 days, or one habit as a 4-week strip or a monthly calendar.
• Tap a row to log today. Done habits log in a single tap without opening the app.
• Sits clear on your wallpaper or on a card, and follows light and dark mode.
• Designed to look at home on minimal launchers such as Niagara.

In the app, log any day, fix a past one from the month view, and see totals, averages and the mix of your choices.

Private by design: everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics and no network access.
